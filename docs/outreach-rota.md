## On each account, in this order

1. **Reply first.** Answer every new comment on our own posts since that account's last day. Replies are the cheapest reach there is.
2. **Then comment out.** Leave a few real comments on recent posts from accounts in the niche: students, study creators, societies. Say something specific to the post. No links, no "check out Offload".
3. **Then follow** a handful of accounts that fit the niche.

## Rules

- Comments follow `brand/safety.md`. On GCSE and A-level, the audience is under 18: comment on posts only, never DM, nothing personal.
- Never comment as if you were a student or a customer. The account is Offload, and it says so.
- Never comment on someone's health, body or grades. On the time account, never name a condition.
- No price, ever. If someone asks, reply "Link in bio" (or "Search Offload" on GCSE).
- Keep a light touch on TikTok: real comments at a human pace, no copy-paste, nothing automated. Bursts of identical activity get accounts restricted.
- Note anything worth turning into a film (a question that keeps coming up, a joke that landed) on the desk's Ideas page.
