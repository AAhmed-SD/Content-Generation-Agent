# A-level Physics: spec map

Every spec point of the five England A-level Physics specifications, in each board's own order, with the notes file that covers it. All five teach the DfE subject content for A-level Physics, so each concept is written once, in the order of AQA 7408 (files 01 to 29), and files 30 to 35 add what other boards examine and AQA does not.

- AQA 7408: numbered sections 3.1.1 to 3.13.6.4, plus required practicals RP1 to RP12. Sections 3.1 to 3.5 (and RP1 to RP6) are also AS (7407); 3.6 to 3.13 are A-level only. Students take one option from 3.9 Astrophysics, 3.10 Medical physics, 3.11 Engineering physics, 3.12 Turning points in physics, 3.13 Electronics.
- Pearson Edexcel 9PH0: Topics 1 to 13, with statements numbered 1 to 191 through the whole specification, plus core practicals CP1 to CP16. Topics 1 to 5 (statements 1 to 96, CP1 to CP8) are also AS (8PH0).
- OCR H556 (Physics A): Modules 1 to 6, lettered points such as 3.1.1(a). Modules 1 to 4 are also AS (H156). Practical Activity Groups PAG1 to PAG12.
- OCR H557 (Physics B, Advancing Physics): Modules 1 to 6. Each section has parts (a) describe and explain, (b) terms and graphs, (c) calculations, (d) practical work, with numbered items, written here as 3.1.1(c)(iv). Module 2 (Fundamental data analysis) is written 2(a)(i). Modules 1 to 4 are also AS (H157). OCR has announced that H557 is being withdrawn: last A-level assessment June 2028.
- WJEC Eduqas A420QS: Component 1 Newtonian physics (C1.1 to C1.8), Component 2 Electricity and the universe (C2.1 to C2.8), Component 3 Light, nuclei and options (C3.1 to C3.10), and one option: A Alternating currents (OA), B Medical physics (OB), C The physics of sports (OC), D Energy and the environment (OD). The spec numbers topics within each component; the C and O prefixes are added here so codes are unique. AS (B420QS) content: C1.1 to C1.4, C2.1 to C2.3, C2.5, C2.7, C3.1 to C3.5 and C3.7.

Level key: AS & A = also in that board's AS; A-level only = not in AS.

`refs_checked: false`: references were taken from the boards' specification PDFs (AQA 7407/7408 version 1.3, Edexcel 9PH0 Issue 3, OCR H556 version 3.0, OCR H557 version 2.9, Eduqas A420QS version 3) and still need a person to check them. Content titles in the tables follow the boards' topic headings or are short paraphrases, not the boards' wording of individual points. Where a point is covered in more than one file, every file is listed, in file order.
