# AQA A-level Environmental Science 7447: spec map

Every spec point of AQA A-level Environmental Science (7447), in the board's order, with the notes file that covers it.

- Specification: first teaching September 2017, first award June 2019. Linear; no non-exam assessment.
- The subject content is numbered 3.1 to 3.7, down to four levels in places (for example 3.4.3.2.7 Oil pollution). This map lists every numbered point. The "Opportunities for skills development" sub-sections (3.1.4, 3.2.1.4, 3.2.2.6, 3.2.3.8, 3.2.4.5, 3.2.5.4, 3.3.5, 3.4.4, 3.5.1.6, 3.5.2.4, 3.5.3.4, 3.6.5, 3.7.3) only list maths (MS) and practical (PS, Me, ST) skill codes; they are covered where the skill is used and gathered in file 23.
- Required practical work (Appendix A): no fixed list of experiments. Every student needs first-hand experience of the six methodologies Me1 to Me6 and the six sampling techniques ST1 to ST6, and at least four days of fieldwork (or two field days plus twelve lab activities). These are covered in [23 Research methods](23-research-methods-and-fieldwork.md), and each technique is also tagged **Practical** where a topic uses it.
- Existing notes elsewhere give background, not a replacement: [A-level Biology 14](../../biology/14-species-taxonomy-and-biodiversity.md) (biodiversity indices), [17](../../biology/17-energy-in-ecosystems-and-nutrient-cycles.md) (energy transfer, nitrogen and phosphorus cycles), [23](../../biology/23-populations-in-ecosystems.md) (succession, sampling, populations) and [42](../../biology/42-climate-change-and-human-impact.md) (climate change); [A-level Chemistry 35](../../chemistry/35-atmosphere-ozone-and-green-chemistry.md) (ozone chemistry); [A-level Physics 18](../../physics/18-radioactivity-decay-and-nuclear-radius.md) (radioactive decay); and the Geology notes in [../geology-ocr-h414](../geology-ocr-h414/map.md) (mineral deposits, exploration, groundwater). The Environmental Science points are still covered in full here, at the depth AQA asks for.
- AS: AQA says the A-level is co-teachable with an AS, but the AS assessment was not in the pages read, so no AS marking is given here (unconfirmed).
- `refs_checked: false`: references were taken from AQA's online specification pages (the PDF link returned an error) and still need a person to check them against the PDF. Short titles below are paraphrases.

## Papers

| Paper | What it examines |
| --- | --- |
| Paper 1 (written, 3 h, 120 marks, 50%) | 3.2 The physical environment, 3.3 Energy resources, 3.4 Pollution, 3.7 Research methods |
| Paper 2 (written, 3 h, 120 marks, 50%) | 3.1 The living environment, 3.5 Biological resources, 3.6 Sustainability, 3.7 Research methods |

Both papers mix multiple choice, short answer and extended writing, and may draw on the whole course. At least 10% of marks are maths (at least higher-tier GCSE level) and at least 15% assess practical knowledge.
