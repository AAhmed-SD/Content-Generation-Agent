# OCR A-level Geology H414: spec map

Every learning outcome of OCR A Level Geology (H414), in the board's order, with the notes file that covers it.

- Specification: version 3.6, March 2026 (first teaching 2017). Qualification number 603/0782/1. OCR is withdrawing the qualification: the last first-teaching cohort started in September 2026 and the last A-level exams are in summer 2028, with no resits.
- Content is in seven modules. Learning outcomes are numbered by section and letter, for example 3.2.1(b)(iii). Modules 1 to 4 (except 1.2, the Practical Endorsement) are also the AS (H014); modules 5 to 7 are A-level only. The Level column below says which.
- Practical Endorsement: at least 12 assessed practicals covering Practical Activity Groups PAG1 to PAG12, and at least four days of fieldwork. PAGs are listed in [28](28-practical-skills-fieldwork-and-pags.md) and tagged in each file where the spec points to them.
- Shared files: WJEC Eduqas A-level Geology (A480QS) covers much of the same ground, so files 01 to 27 here are written once for both boards and list both in their front matter. The Eduqas map is [../geology-eduqas-a480qs/map.md](../geology-eduqas-a480qs/map.md).
- Background at a lower level: [GCSE Geology (Eduqas C480QS)](../../../gcse/smaller-stem/geology-eduqas/map.md). Radioactive decay maths: [A-level Physics 18](../../physics/18-radioactivity-decay-and-nuclear-radius.md).
- `refs_checked: false`: references were taken from the version 3.6 spec PDF and still need a person to check them. Short titles below are paraphrases.
