# AQA GCSE Combined Science: Synergy (8465): spec map

Every subject content section of AQA GCSE Combined Science: Synergy, 8465, in AQA's order, with the notes that cover it. Synergy teaches the same government combined science content as Trilogy (8464), but mixes biology, chemistry and physics in eight integrated topics (4.1 Building blocks to 4.8 Guiding Spaceship Earth towards a sustainable future), plus 4.9 Key ideas. Papers 1 and 2 are life and environmental sciences; papers 3 and 4 are physical sciences.

The links go to the AQA GCSE Biology, Chemistry and Physics notes in `notes/aqa/gcse/` (the section named in each link). Synergy section titles are often the same as the separate-science ones; where a Synergy section draws on several separate-science sections, each is listed. Where Synergy asks for something the separate-science notes do not cover, the row links to [integrated-topics.md](integrated-topics.md).

How to read the table:

- **Ref**: AQA's section number, such as 4.1.3.2, or RP1 to RP21 for the 21 required practical activities (AQA numbers them 1 to 21 in the subject content).
- **Flags**: HT = Higher tier only. Synergy only includes Trilogy-level content, so the "(Biology only)", "(Chemistry only)" and "(Physics only)" tags in the linked notes do not apply, except where a row links to that section on purpose (black body radiation for RP6, orbital motion for circular motion, plant diseases and Rf values in chromatography); read those sections only as far as the Synergy row needs.

`refs_checked: false`: section numbers and titles were taken from the AQA specification PDF (Version 1.4, October 2026) and still need a person to check them. Only section headings are reproduced.

Gap file: [integrated-topics.md](integrated-topics.md): plant pigments and chromatography (RP9), light emission by atoms, the water cycle, Earth's magnetism, circular motion, and the 4.9 key ideas.
