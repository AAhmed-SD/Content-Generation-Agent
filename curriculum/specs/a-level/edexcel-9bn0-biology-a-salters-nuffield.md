## About this specification

Every spec point of the six England A-level Biology specifications, in each board's own order, with the notes file that covers it. All six teach the DfE subject content for A-level Biology, so each concept is written once, in the order of AQA 7402, and files 26 to 49 add what other boards examine and AQA does not.

- AQA 7402: numbered sections 3.1.1 to 3.8.4.3, plus required practicals RP1 to RP12. Sections 3.1 to 3.4 (and RP1 to RP6) are also AS (7401); 3.5 to 3.8 are A-level only.
- Pearson Edexcel 9BN0 (Biology A, Salters-Nuffield): Topics 1 to 8, spec points 1.1 to 8.19, plus core practicals CP1 to CP18. Topics 1 to 4 are also AS (8BN0).
- Pearson Edexcel 9BI0 (Biology B): Topics 1 to 10, written here as subtopic plus numeral (1.1 i), plus core practicals CP1 to CP16. Topics 1 to 4 are also AS (8BI0).
- OCR H420 (Biology A): Modules 1 to 6, lettered points such as 2.1.1(a). Modules 1 to 4 are also AS (H020). Practical activity groups (PAG1 to PAG12) are in file 26.
- OCR H422 (Biology B, Advancing Biology): Modules 1 to 5, lettered points such as 2.1.1(a). Modules 1 to 3 are also AS (H022).
- WJEC Eduqas A400QS: Core concepts (written CC1 to CC5), Component 1 Energy for life (C1.1 to C1.6), Component 2 Continuity of life (C2.1 to C2.7), Component 3 Requirements for life (C3.1 to C3.5) and one option: A Immunology and disease (OA), B Human musculoskeletal anatomy (OB), C Neurobiology and behaviour (OC). The spec numbers topics within each component; the CC/C/O prefixes are added here so codes are unique.

Level key: AS & A = also in that board's AS; A-level only = not in AS.
