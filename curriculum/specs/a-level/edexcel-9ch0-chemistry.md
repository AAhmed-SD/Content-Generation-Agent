## About this specification

Every spec point of the five England A-level Chemistry specifications, in each board's own order, with the notes file that covers it. All five teach the DfE subject content for A-level Chemistry, so each concept is written once, in the order of AQA 7405 (files 01 to 31), and files 32 to 37 add practical skills and content that other boards examine and AQA does not.

- AQA 7405: numbered sections 3.1.1.1 to 3.3.16, plus required practicals RP1 to RP12. Sections 3.1.1 to 3.1.7, 3.2.1 to 3.2.3 and 3.3.1 to 3.3.6 (and RP1 to RP6) are also AS (7404); the rest is A-level only.
- Pearson Edexcel 9CH0: Topics 1 to 19, numbered spec points written as topic.point (1.1 to 19.8; sub-topic letters such as 6A or 17B are shown in the Content column), plus core practicals CP1 to CP16. Topics 1 to 10 are also AS (8CH0).
- OCR H432 (Chemistry A): Modules 1 to 6, lettered learning outcomes such as 2.1.1(a). Modules 1 to 4 are also AS (H032). Practical activity groups PAG1 to PAG12 sit inside the topic files.
- OCR H433 (Chemistry B, Salters): Module 1 (practical skills, 1.1.1 to 1.2.2) and ten storylines with lettered outcomes: Elements of life (EL), Developing fuels (DF), Elements from the sea (ES), The ozone story (OZ), What's in a medicine? (WM), The chemical industry (CI), Polymers and life (PL), Oceans (O), Developing metals (DM), Colour by design (CD). EL to WM are also AS (H033).
- WJEC Eduqas A410QS: Core concepts C1 to C3 (also AS, B410QS), Physical and inorganic chemistry PI1 to PI5, and Organic chemistry and analysis OA1 to OA4, with lettered statements such as C1.2(a). Specified practical work is listed under each topic, numbered here as SP1, SP2 and so on in spec order (the SP numbers are added here so each row is unique). Component 3 is a practical examination.

Level key: AS & A = also in that board's AS; A-level only = not in AS.
