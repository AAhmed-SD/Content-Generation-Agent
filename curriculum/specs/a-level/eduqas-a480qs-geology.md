## About this specification

Every spec point of WJEC Eduqas A level Geology (A480QS), in the board's order, with the notes file that covers it.

- Specification: version 2, October 2019 (first teaching 2017, first award 2019).
- Content is in three sections: Fundamentals of Geology (F1 to F4), Interpreting the Geological Record (G1 to G4) and Geological Themes (T1 and T2 compulsory, plus one option from T3, T4 and T5). Each topic has numbered Key Ideas with lettered points in a "knowledge and understanding" column and a matching "techniques and skills" column; both are examined. This map writes "F2.3d" for topic F2, Key Idea 3, point d.
- AS: the Fundamentals section (F1 to F4) is the whole of the Eduqas AS Geology content. G and T topics are A-level only.
- Specified practicals SP1 to SP20 (Appendix B) are needed for the Practical Endorsement; they are tagged where they appear and gathered in [06](../../notes/a-level/smaller-stem/geology-eduqas-a480qs/06-specified-practicals-and-fieldwork.md). At least four days of fieldwork are required, with a fieldwork statement sent to WJEC by 15 May of the year of the award.
- Shared files: most content overlaps OCR A-level Geology (H414). Those points are covered by the shared files in [../geology-ocr-h414/](../../notes/a-level/smaller-stem/geology-ocr-h414/map.md), which list both boards in their front matter. Files in this folder cover what only Eduqas examines.
- Background at a lower level: [GCSE Geology (Eduqas C480QS)](../../notes/gcse/smaller-stem/geology-eduqas/map.md).
- `refs_checked: false`: references were taken from the version 2 spec PDF and still need a person to check them. Short titles below are paraphrases.
