## About this specification

Every spec point of the four boards, in each board's own order, with the notes file that covers it. All four specifications teach the same DfE core content for A-level Further Mathematics (Core Pure), so each concept is written once; option papers that overlap (statistics, mechanics, decision or discrete, further pure) also share files. Further Maths assumes all of A-level Mathematics; those notes are in [../maths/](../../notes/a-level/maths/map.md) and are linked rather than repeated.

- AQA 7367 uses the DfE codes for its compulsory content (A1 to J3, after the overarching themes OT1 to OT3) and adds letters for its three optional applications: mechanics (MA to ME), statistics (SA to SH) and discrete (DA to DG). Students take the compulsory content and two of the three options.
- Pearson Edexcel 9FM0 has Core Pure on Papers 1 and 2 (topics 1.1 to 9.9, written here as CP1.1 to CP9.9), then two option papers chosen from Further Pure 1 and 2 (FP1, FP2), Further Statistics 1 and 2 (FS1, FS2), Further Mechanics 1 and 2 (FM1, FM2) and Decision Mathematics 1 and 2 (D1, D2). Option refs are written with the paper first, for example FP1 2.3 or D2 5.4.
- OCR H245 (Further Mathematics A) has Pure Core (4.01a to 4.10h, papers Y540 and Y541) and two of Statistics (5.xx, Y542), Mechanics (6.xx, Y543), Discrete Mathematics (7.xx, Y544) and Additional Pure Mathematics (8.xx, Y545). Its Stage 1 column is shared with AS H235; Stage 2 is A-level only.
- OCR MEI H645 (Further Mathematics B) has Core Pure (Y420) and options worth 180 marks in total: one major option (Mechanics Major Y421 or Statistics Major Y422) and one minor, or three minors (Mechanics Minor Y431, Statistics Minor Y432, Modelling with Algorithms Y433, Numerical Methods Y434, Extra Pure Y435, Further Pure with Technology Y436). The spec prints a code's full letters only on the first row of each table (for example Pj1, then j2); every code here carries them. Tables headed (a) are shared with AS H635; tables headed (b) are A-level only. Modelling with Algorithms and Numerical Methods are also AS papers (Y413, Y414). Extra Pure and Further Pure with Technology are A-level only here (not confirmed against the H635 spec). Further Pure with Technology (Y436) is being withdrawn: last first teaching September 2026, last exam summer 2028. The minor options reuse the major options' codes for the content they share.

Level key: AS & A = also in AS Further Mathematics; A-level only = not in AS; AS & A (part A-level only) = the AS course has the first part, and the rest is tagged in the notes; Option = an optional paper (see the board notes above for which are also offered at AS).
