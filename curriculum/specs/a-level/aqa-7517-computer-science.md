## About this specification

Every examined spec point of the three England A-level Computer Science specifications, in each board's own order, with the notes file that covers it. Pearson Edexcel has no A-level Computer Science. All three boards teach the DfE subject content for A-level Computer Science, so each concept is written once, in the order of AQA 7517 (files 01 to 33). Files 34 to 37 add content that OCR and Eduqas examine and AQA does not. Where the GCSE notes already cover the basics, files link to [../../gcse/computer-science/](../../notes/gcse/computer-science/map.md) and build on them.

The coursework (non-exam assessment) is not covered: AQA 4.14 (the computing practical project), OCR components 03/04 (programming project) and Eduqas Component 3 (programmed solution to a problem).

- AQA 7517: numbered subject content 4.1 to 4.13 (to four levels, for example 4.1.1.1; sections 4.8, 4.10 and 4.11 are numbered to three levels only). The AS (7516) numbers the shared content 3.1 to 3.9; the Level column shows which A-level points are also in the AS.
- OCR H446: components 01 (Computer systems, 1.1 to 1.5) and 02 (Algorithms and programming, 2.1 to 2.3), with lettered learning outcomes such as 1.2.1(d). The AS (H046) numbers its content differently; the Level column shows which outcomes it also contains ("Part AS" where the AS has only some of the outcome).
- WJEC Eduqas A500QS: Component 1 (Programming and system development, topics 1 to 9) and Component 2 (Computer architecture, data, communication and applications, topics 1 to 8). The specification lists content statements under unnumbered side headings, so references here are "component.topic: side heading", for example "C1.3: Sorting"; statements with no side heading use the topic title. The AS (B500QS) has been withdrawn (final award summer 2027), so Eduqas rows have no level column.

`refs_checked: false`: references were taken from the AQA 7516/7517 specification PDF (version 1.6, 31 July 2023), the Eduqas A-level specification PDF (Version 2, January 2019) and, for OCR, the H446 numbering cross-checked against the OCR AS H046 specification PDF (Version 2.0, February 2026). The H446 PDF itself could not be downloaded (it is over 10 MB), so OCR's lettered outcomes still need a person to check them against the H446 PDF. Short titles in the tables are headings or paraphrases, not the boards' wording.
