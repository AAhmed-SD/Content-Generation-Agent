## About this specification

Every assessable learning outcome of OCR GCSE (9–1) Chemistry B (Twenty First Century Science), J258, in OCR's order, with the notes that cover it. Six chapters (C1 to C6) of question-led sub-topics with numbered outcomes; Chapter C7 is Ideas about Science and Chapter C8 is practical skills (eight PAGs). Lettered parts of an outcome are covered by the same row.

The links go to the AQA GCSE notes in `notes/aqa/gcse/` (the section named in each link) or to the gap files in this folder, which hold only what the AQA notes lack. Where a spec point is wider than one AQA section, every section it needs is listed.

How to read the table:

- **Ref**: OCR's learning outcome reference, for example B1.1.1 (sub-topic B1.1, outcome 1). The topic and sub-topic come from the reference.
- **Sub-topic**: OCR's sub-topic heading.
- **Flags**: HT = Higher tier only (OCR prints these statements in bold). Separate only = not in Combined Science B (J260); OCR marks these "separate science only".
- **IaS**: Ideas about Science outcomes (IaS1.1 to IaS4.6), taught through every chapter. They are the same in all four OCR B specifications and are covered once, in [ideas-about-science.md](../../notes/gcse/ocr-b-sciences/ideas-about-science.md).
- **Practical activity groups (PAGs)**: OCR's eight groups of practical apparatus and techniques for this subject, listed at the end. Each links to the practical guide in this folder and to the AQA required practical that uses the same techniques.
