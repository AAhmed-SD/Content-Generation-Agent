## About this specification

Every learning outcome of OCR GCSE (9–1) Chemistry A (Gateway Science), J248, in OCR's order, with the notes that cover it. Six teaching topics (C1 to C6) with sub-topics and lettered outcomes; Topic C7 is practical skills, assessed through the eight chemistry PAGs.

The links go to the AQA GCSE notes in `notes/aqa/gcse/` (the section named in each link) or to the gap files in this folder, which hold only what the AQA notes lack. Where a spec point is wider than one AQA section, every section it needs is listed.

How to read the table:

- **Ref**: OCR's learning outcome reference, for example B1.1a. The topic and sub-topic come from the reference.
- **Sub-topic**: OCR's sub-topic heading.
- **Flags**: HT = Higher tier only (OCR prints these statements in bold). Separate only = not in Combined Science A (J250).
- **Practical activity groups (PAGs)**: OCR's eight groups of practical apparatus and techniques for this subject, listed at the end. Each links to the practical guide in this folder and to the AQA required practical that uses the same techniques.

`refs_checked: false`: references, tiers and the separate-only flags were taken from the OCR specification PDF (Version 4.0, August 2026) and still need a person to check them. Separate-only flags were found by matching each outcome against the Combined Science A specification. Sub-topic titles are OCR's headings; nothing else of OCR's wording is reproduced.
