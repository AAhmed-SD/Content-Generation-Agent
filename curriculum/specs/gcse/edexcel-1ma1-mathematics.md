## About this specification

Every spec point of the four boards, in each board's own order, with the notes file that covers it. AQA 8300 and Edexcel 1MA1 use the DfE subject content codes (N1 to S6) unchanged. OCR J560 uses its own numbering. Eduqas C300QS has a Foundation list (FN, FA, FR, FG, FP, FS) and a Higher list (HN, HA, HR, HG, HP, HS) whose numbers differ from the DfE codes in Algebra, Ratio, Geometry and Statistics.

Tier key: F & H = on both tiers; F & H (part Higher only) = the point is on both tiers but part of it is Higher only (tagged (HT only) in the notes); Higher only = not on the Foundation tier.

`refs_checked: false`: references and tiers were taken from the boards' specifications (AQA website subject content; Edexcel, OCR and Eduqas spec PDFs) and still need a person to check them.
