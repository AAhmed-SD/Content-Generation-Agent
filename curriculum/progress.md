## Scope: England STEM, GCSE and A-level

Decided 2026-11-09: build STEM only, for the four England boards (AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC Eduqas). WJEC (Wales) and CCEA (Northern Ireland) are out for now. Essay-based subjects wait until this is done.

Core STEM: Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Combined Science, Maths, Further Maths, Statistics, Computer Science.

Smaller STEM, after the core: Engineering (AQA GCSE), Astronomy (Edexcel GCSE), Electronics and Geology (Eduqas, OCR), Environmental Science (AQA A-level).
