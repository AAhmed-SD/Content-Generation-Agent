# Sound

The goal film has a music bed for almost the whole forty-five seconds. That recording is not ours. Do not lift it.

This desk makes two kinds of sound, and they stay separate.

Kokoro speaks a card’s narration into a wav beside the video. British voice `bf_emma`. That wav is not mixed into the picture. Run it from the card when a person wants the line read aloud.

The score is hits on the cuts. No licensed music. The words stay readable with the sound off. `node scripts/score.mjs` reads the cue list below and writes `remotion/public/goal-score.wav`.

## Kinds

- click: a short interface tick when a mark lands.
- impact: a low hit when a new field takes the frame.
- whoosh: a short noise sweep when a shape enters.
- tick: the clock, three times, then stop.
- tone: a soft note when a line finishes, not a chord, not a song.
