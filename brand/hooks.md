# Hooks

Write the thing a person does. A save, a finish, a card kept. A feeling is not a hook.

## Behaviour over feeling

The line names an action the viewer can picture: filing one finding, labeling one figure, keeping one sentence, marking the line they missed. It does not name a mood, a transformation, or a promise about how they will feel.

## Formats

These are the four pieces the desk already schedules. Timings come from the OffloadCard schema.

- Hook. A video, six seconds. One sentence. The job is the stop.
- Payoff. A video, six seconds, the same day. The job is the line worth saving.
- Still. One card. The job is the screenshot.
- Carousel. Three beats, nine seconds. The job is reaching the third card.

## Proven hooks

No hook in the desk has a stored view count, completion, or save count. Do not invent a number, a “winner”, or a format that worked. When the loop has a 7-day row, this section is updated from that row and from nowhere else.
