# Identity

Offload looks like paper that has been taken out of a drawer. Cream ground, black ink, one object, one sentence. The word offload is the only blue, and it is written in script. Red is a stamp, used when a person has approved the card. Kraft appears when the object is a folder. A black ground is reserved for a post that is withholding something.

## Look

- Warm off-white paper, near-black ink.
- One blue: the word offload.
- Red only as a stamp.
- Motion is eased paper leaving a drawer. Nothing spins, nothing flashes.
- The design is finished. The words change. The objects do not.

## Objects

The drawer, the folder, the index card, the calendar, the key, the calculator. Each niche keeps one of these and repeats it.

## Voice

Short enough to read aloud in one breath. Script for the human line. A plain sentence for the fact. No slogan voice, no hostess voice, no iced-coffee caption, no “link in bio” patter.

## Accounts

Four accounts, one brand. offload.clinical, offload.analytical, offload.mentalhealth, offload.gcsealevel. Each account has its own object and its own rule. They share the paper.
