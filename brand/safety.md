# Safety

Every film needs a person's approval before it posts (one admin is enough). GCSE and A-level is read by people under 18. Nothing posts without a yes.

## Clinical

The account advertises Offload to students in medicine, dentistry, nursing, and radiology. It relates to revision. It is not clinical advice. No diagnosis. No cure. No patient. No case.

## Mental health

The account is for students with ADHD and other neurodivergence, including people under 18. Films name the struggle with schoolwork and explain the executive function behind it. No diagnosis of the viewer. No treatment claim. No “you have”. If the line is distressing, the caption points to help and does not continue the story. Comments are not turned into cases.

## Analytical

The account advertises Offload to students in maths, economics, and computer science. It relates to unfinished work. It does not work the question and it does not invent a number.

## Under 18

GCSE and A-level posts are educational. Syllabus-true. Age-appropriate. No student names. No commenter profiles. Comments are stored as themes only. No sexual or romantic bait. No fake grade.

## Ambassadors

An ambassador post begins with the word Ad. A post that is not an ambassador post does not say Ad. Do not invent an ambassador.

## Banned words

## Neurodivergent students (updated 2026-11-07)

Neurodivergent students (ADHD, autism, dyslexia, executive-function difficulties) are the time account's audience. Its films may and should name the condition and the specific study struggle, and explain the executive function behind it (working memory, task initiation, time perception, planning) accurately and kindly, then show how Offload carries that load. They talk about the condition ("with ADHD, ..."), never about the viewer: never "you have ADHD", never assess or diagnose, never promise to treat or fix anything, no medication talk, no invented statistics, and never make the condition the punchline. The clinical, analytical and GCSE accounts do not target neurodivergence.

The research filter drops a theme that contains any of these: cure, guaranteed, diagnos, suicide, kill myself, onlyfans, escort, nude, porn, hostess. The writer does not put them back.
