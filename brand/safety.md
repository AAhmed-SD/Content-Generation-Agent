# Safety

Clinical and mental health need both stamps before a pack can open. GCSE and A-level is read by people under 18. Nothing posts without a yes.

## Clinical

The account advertises Offload to students in medicine, dentistry, nursing, and radiology. It relates to revision. It is not clinical advice. No diagnosis. No cure. No patient. No case.

## Mental health

The account is for kids and young people with ADHD and other mental health difficulties, including people under 18. The post is about managing time. No diagnosis. No treatment claim. No “you have”. If the line is distressing, the caption points to help and does not continue the story. Comments are not turned into cases.

## Analytical

The account advertises Offload to students in maths, economics, and computer science. It relates to unfinished work. It does not work the question and it does not invent a number.

## Under 18

GCSE and A-level posts are educational. Syllabus-true. Age-appropriate. No student names. No commenter profiles. Comments are stored as themes only. No sexual or romantic bait. No fake grade.

## Ambassadors

An ambassador post begins with the word Ad. A post that is not an ambassador post does not say Ad. Do not invent an ambassador.

## Banned words

The research filter drops a theme that contains any of these: cure, guaranteed, diagnos, suicide, kill myself, onlyfans, escort, nude, porn, hostess. The writer does not put them back.
