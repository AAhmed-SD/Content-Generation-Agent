# Motion

This is the picture for the fast film. Claude names a move. It does not write easing, opacity curves, or frame numbers. The tuned pieces live in `remotion/src/motion`. The film component is `remotion/src/FastIssues.tsx`.

## Timing

- An entrance uses a spring, damping 12, mass 0.6. It is settled in about 8 to 12 frames.
- A cut is a whip. Six frames, a slide from the right, and blur. The whip is faster than the entrance.
- Words in a line arrive 2 frames apart. Opacity never moves on its own. It moves with a rise.
- The camera scales from 1 to 1.04 across the whole film, so a line that has landed is still drifting.
- A shape keeps a slow drift after it lands. A beat is not a still card.
- Words land when they are spoken. The caption is built from word timestamps, not from a block fade. A filler word is pale. A plain word is bold sans. The emotional word is italic serif, larger, in solid blue.
- A scene may stay up for two or three seconds only while the object keeps moving. The hero scales from 1 to 0.92 across that hold. A new picture still arrives about every second.
- One drawn object sits in the middle of a pale shape. Photos are run through the blue duotone. Nothing keeps its original colours. There is no trial line and no invented count.
- Move names, and only these: cross, grid, circle, wedge, squares, corners.
