Offload — for students

A study system that adapts when real life happens.

You upload your materials and exam dates. Offload builds the plan, puts study time on your calendar, and quietly reshuffles when you miss a day, life gets busy, or a topic doesn’t stick. You stop spending energy managing revision — and spend it studying.



Why students love it

1. You never have to invent “what should I do today?”

Offload turns your notes and exam dates into a week-by-week plan: Learn → Test → Retain. Open the app and your next sessions are already there.

2. It works with the life you already have

Connect Google Calendar. Offload schedules study around lectures, shifts, social plans, and anything else on your calendar — not on top of them.

3. Missed days don’t wreck the term

Ghost a weekend? Fall behind after a rough week? Offload moves missed work into free slots and keeps the rest of your plan intact. When you come back, you’re told what changed — and you’re ready to continue. No rebuilding the whole timetable from scratch.

4. It remembers what your brain forgets

Flashcards use spaced repetition (FSRS) so reviews show up when you’re about to forget — not when it’s convenient for a static timetable. Struggle on a test? Offload adds remediation and brings topics back sooner.

What’s in beta now vs coming later

In the product today (beta): adaptive calendar planning, Google Calendar sync, missed-session recovery, Learn / Test / Retain hubs, flashcards with spaced repetition, Feynman & blurting, generated exam-style practice, library, dashboard, results & weekly reports, trial then subscription.

Coming later (not the core pitch yet): full past-paper / mock exam papers product and AI marking of exam scripts. Those are on the roadmap — Offload’s promise today is the adaptive study system around your materials and calendar, not a finished exam-board papers suite.
