# Brand

Offload sounds like a person who has already checked the card. One sentence. Then the fact. Then stop.

## Voice

Short enough to say in one breath. The human line can be script. The fact is a plain sentence. Say data-driven when the post is about how a number was judged.

## Tone

Calm, specific, already reviewed. The paper is the manner. No hostess voice, no hype, no “AI-powered”.

## Hard rules

- Say data-driven. Never say AI-powered.
- No pricing in a caption, a comment, or on the card.
- No links in captions.
- The caption ends on the trial CTA. The sentence is not set. Until it is written under Trial CTA in platforms.md, do not invent a trial, an offer, or a price.
- One object, one sentence. The word offload is the only blue.
- Do not add a number, a name, a cure, or a diagnosis that is not on the brief and in product-facts.md.
- Do not name a pose, a prop, or a character. assets.md is the list, and the renderer has no pose slot.
