# GCSE and A-level

This account advertises Offload to GCSE and A-level students, including people under 18. The post relates to revision. It does not invent a grade.

## Line

The paper has a date.

## Goal

A student should save the card because the revision is too much to hold at once. The post is an advertisement, and it stays syllabus-true when it names the paper.

## Audience

Students sitting GCSE and A-level, including people under 18.

## Pains

A specification that does not fit in one evening. A tip that is not on the syllabus. A named student.

## Vocabulary

Paper, revision, card, mark scheme. The exam name on this account is AQA, and only when the brief already uses it.

## Exams

AQA.

## Rules

Syllabus-true. Age-appropriate. No student names. Comments stay themes. No sexual or romantic bait. No fake grade. Do not invent a mark. Laiba stamps it. Do not describe how Offload works.

## UK context (researched 2026-11-05)

Background for the team and the writer. **Never put these numbers on a card or in a film** unless the week's brief carries them. Use them to pick true, timely subjects.

### Who they are

Year 10 and 11 (GCSE) and Year 12 and 13 (A-level) in England and Wales, many under 18, plus post-16 students resitting English or maths. Boards: AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR, WJEC and Eduqas. Name a board only if the brief does.

### Their exams, and when

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| GCSE and A-level summer exams | **Monday 10 May to Friday 18 June 2027** (JCQ common timetable), with a contingency day on 23 June. GCSE results day is in August. |
| November resits (English Language and maths only) | Early November 2026. Results on 14 January 2027. |
| Mocks (Year 11 and Year 13) | November to January in many schools. |

### A typical day

- School from about 8:30 to 3:30, five or six lessons.
- Homework, then revision in the evening, if the phone allows.
- Weekends: sport, a part-time job for some sixth-formers, and "I'll do a big session on Sunday".
- In Year 11 and Year 13, every subject feels urgent at once.

The thread: **too much spec, not enough evenings, and a plan that dies the first time they miss one.**

### What is true right now

- **Resits are happening now.** Post-16 students without a grade 4 in English or maths must keep studying them.
- **Mock season is starting.**
- **Extra time is common.** Ofqual's 2024 to 2025 figures put 25% extra time at between 16.6% and 25.5% of students. Never assume who has it. Never use it as a label.
