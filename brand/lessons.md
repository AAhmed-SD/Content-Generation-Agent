## Rules for the picture and the words

1. Every line must match what the picture shows; check the script against the spec before sending (editor: 3 contradictions in 1 script, clinical "The Saturday bank shift").
2. Don't narrate a change if the picture ends where it began, e.g. blocks "shuffled" when each lands back on its own day (editor, 1 script).
3. Don't claim time or effort lost, like "an evening gone", unless the picture shows it being spent (editor, 1 script).
4. Don't say "only" or "the one free day" until you've checked the spec for other open slots (editor, 1 script).
5. In chat films, show the POV first, before any message text (1 send-back, gcsealevel).
6. Keep the POV clear of the messages, including when new messages push the thread up (1 send-back, gcsealevel).
