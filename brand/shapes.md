## versus

Versus: two ways side by side (cram vs spaced, highlight vs test, tabs vs one question), and which wins.

### The blind test (`versus-blind-test`)

Use when the losing method looks more productive than the winner. The two are shown without labels and judged only on what they leave behind, so the viewer learns that looking busy and learning are different things.

| When | Job | Voice | Screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0-2s | Set up an unlabelled comparison | Example: 'Two revision methods. No names. Judge by results.' | Two covered panels marked 'Method 1' and 'Method 2' |
| 2-10s | Show what each one looks like, one detail at a time | Neutral description. Example: 'One fills three pages.' 'One fills half a page, mostly crossings out.' | Method 1 builds a neat, full page. Method 2 is a messy half page with red marks |
| 10-16s | Run the same test on both | Example: 'Same question, a week later.' | One exam-style question drops onto both panels. Method 1 gets a blank; Method 2 gets a tick |
| 16-21s | Remove the covers to reveal the labels | The turn, kept short. Example: 'Method 1 was highlighting.' | The covers lift to show the labels, for example 'Highlighting' against 'Testing yourself' |
| 21-29s | Product beats tied to the messy winner | Example: 'Offload does the messy one with you. It tests you, and keeps what you got wrong.' | The app's question screen in the same half-page style, with a miss logged |
| 29-33s | Ending, then the end card | A punchline. Example: 'Ugly page. Good method.' | The two pages are held side by side, then the Offload end card |

Hook styles: Quiz, Hot take, Myth. Formats: scene, split, exampaper, whiteboard. Accounts: clinical, analytical, gcsealevel.

Example: offload.analytical: a neat page of copied proof steps against a scrappy attempt at the proof from blank, both tested on a fresh problem sheet.

### Your head against a record (`versus-head-vs-record`)

Use when the thing being compared is where something is kept rather than a method. Items go into two lanes, your head and a record, and then a question is asked of both, so the viewer feels the relief of not having to hold it all.

| When | Job | Voice | Screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 20-28s | Product beats, said warm | Example: 'Offload is the record. It keeps every miss. You just show up.' | The record lane becomes the app's dashboard of what's due |
