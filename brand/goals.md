# Goals

Offload is local-business marketing that researches, drafts, scores, and only posts after a person says yes. The four niches are the proof that the same paper system can carry different work.

## The point of a post

A post has one job. If the job is unclear, the card is not ready.

- The hook makes someone stop.
- The payoff gives them the thing worth saving.
- The still is the card they can screenshot.
- The carousel teaches the same idea in three beats.

Reach is a side effect. A save, a finish, and a return the next week are the result the loop is built to see.

## What improvement means

Improvement is a change you can point at. Completion higher at 7 days than at 1 hour. Saves higher than the last time this niche ran the same kind of card. A line you stopped using because the numbers went quiet. The Monday note records the change in a sentence, and the next brief is allowed to use that sentence.
