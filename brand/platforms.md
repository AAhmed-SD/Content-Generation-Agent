# Platforms

The pack builds these captions in code. Lengths are the cuts in `fillCaptions`. No caption contains a link or a price.

## Trial CTA

The last line of a caption is the trial CTA. The sentence is not set. Do not invent one.

## Cross-posting (planned)

One video goes to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube Shorts. It is not rewritten for each one. The script has one body and two hook openers, A and B. The two openers make the same promise in different words. Neither opener may add a claim, a number, or a word that the body does not support.

Every platform gets the clean master. No platform's watermark appears on another platform. No music is named in a script.

## TikTok

`{line} {note}`, cut at 220 characters. The search keyword is the account name already printed on the card. No hashtag is added.

## Instagram

Three lines: the line, the note, then `#offload`. That is the only hashtag.

## YouTube

`{niche label}: {line} {note}`, cut at 200 characters. The account name leads.

## Facebook

`{line} {note} Filed under {account name}.`

## First comment

`Filed this week. Code {CODE}-W45.`

Codes: clinical CLN, analytical ANL, mental health MH, GCSE and A-level GCS. The comment carries the code. It does not carry a link.
